The Idea

Two things Lynnwood was missing.

JD's started with two things this neighborhood didn't have close by: pizza you can trust is halal, and a juice bar to go with it. We're building both under one roof — a hand-tossed crust topped with halal chicken and fresh vegetables, and juice and smoothies blended to order at the same counter. What We Serve

Two sides of the counter.

The Oven Side

Pizza, halal, hot.

Halal pizza made to order — build your own or go with a house favorite, then wait for it hot out of the oven. Hand-tossed dough, halal chicken and veggie toppings, classic to spicy, by the pie or the slice.

The Juice Side

Juice, made at the counter.

Juice and smoothies poured fresh right next to the ovens — the other half of the menu, ready whenever your pizza is. Fruit blends, green juices, and shakes, made to order, no shortcuts.

Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Is everything at JD's halal?

Yes — JD's is a 100% halal kitchen. No pork and no alcohol are served or prepared anywhere on site.

Can I ask for it spicier (or milder)?

Every pizza is made to order, so tell the kitchen how you like it — classic, mild, or turned all the way up.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Veggie toppings are available on any pizza, and the juice bar has plenty for the whole table either way.

How do I order?

We're not taking orders just yet. Call 425-245-8955 with any questions — online ordering for pickup and delivery goes live the day we open.

Do you deliver?

We're planning pickup and delivery through Toast once we open. Check back here or call for the latest.

What are your hours?

Once we open, JD's will run 12:00 PM to 2:00 AM, seven days a week — including late for after-hours cravings.

Where are you located?

4309 198th St SW in Lynnwood, WA. Call 425-245-8955 if you need directions or have questions before we open.

Can I pre-order before you open?

Not yet — online ordering goes live the day we open. Call us in the meantime and we'll make sure you're taken care of.
